# Tier 1 English Sales Playbook

**Market:** United States, Puerto Rico, international English-speaking  
**Date:** 2026-06-14  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Ideal client profile (ICP)

### Who to target
- **Role:** Owner, CEO, COO, Director of Sales/Marketing, VP of Operations
- **Business type:** Premium B2B or B2C with deal values above US$5,000 per transaction
- **Verticals:** Real estate development, professional services, premium healthcare, hospitality, private education, high-ticket e-commerce, financial services
- **Revenue signal:** Has an active sales team OR is a solo high-performer doing $500K+ per year
- **Pain signal:** Losing leads to slow response, no follow-up system, or disorganized CRM
- **Budget signal:** Has invested in advertising before. Is not asking "how much does a website cost"

### Who NOT to pursue
- Startups with no revenue and no budget clarity
- Clients who want to "try something cheap first"
- Clients who compare Luma to Fiverr or template-based agencies
- Anyone whose investment horizon is below US$5,000

## Call opening (cold or warm)

**Version A — after inbound lead:**
> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from Luma Premium. You submitted a commercial assessment request — I wanted to follow up personally. I've reviewed your profile briefly. You're in [industry], and based on what you shared, there are a few things I'd like to understand better before I make a recommendation. Do you have 20 minutes this week?"

**Version B — cold outreach (LinkedIn / email):**
> "Hi [Name] — I noticed [specific observation about their business: ad running, website gap, no follow-up, etc.]. At Luma Premium we design commercial infrastructure for [their vertical]. Not a website — the full capture-to-conversion system. I have a short assessment that takes 4 minutes. Would it be worth a look?"

---

## Discovery questions (assessment call)

1. "Walk me through what happens when a new lead comes in today — from the moment they inquire to the moment you close or lose the deal."
2. "What percentage of your leads do you estimate you're actually following up with consistently?"
3. "Do you know which channel or campaign is bringing your highest-quality leads right now?"
4. "What's your current CRM situation — are you using one, or is it more WhatsApp and spreadsheets?"
5. "What's your biggest commercial frustration right now — not a tech problem, a revenue problem?"
6. "If we fixed [their stated pain] in 90 days, what does that mean for your business in dollar terms?"
7. "What's your decision-making process for an investment like this — is it you, or do you need to loop in someone else?"

## Presenting Luma Premium

**Frame it as infrastructure, not a service:**
> "What we do is different from a web agency. We don't build a website and hand it over. We design the full commercial system: the digital presence, the capture forms, the automated responses, the CRM, the follow-up sequences — all connected. Think of it as the difference between buying a car and building a distribution network."

**Use their pain as the through-line:**
> "You mentioned that leads come in but don't get followed up. That's a system problem, not a people problem. Here's what we'd build to solve that specifically: [walk through relevant OS — CRM, concierge, or real estate OS]."

**Demo guidance:**
- Lead with the problem, not the feature
- Show the demo that matches their vertical (Real Estate OS, Commerce OS, etc.)
- Narrate what the prospect experiences, not what the tech does
- End with: "This is what your operation could look like in 60–90 days."

## Common objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course. What specifically would help you feel confident? Is it the investment, the timeline, or understanding exactly what you'd receive?" |
| "It's too expensive." | "Compared to what? If this system brings in one additional deal per month, what does that mean in revenue? The investment pays for itself in a single close for most of our clients." |
| "I've been burned by agencies before." | "That's exactly why we start with an assessment instead of a proposal. We don't sell until we know we can deliver results for your specific operation." |
| "Can you do it for less?" | "We can discuss scope. What's the most important result for you in the first 90 days? Let's build to that and see what investment makes sense." |
| "I want to see more examples first." | "Absolutely — I'll send you the relevant demos. Which part of the system is most important to see: the capture experience, the CRM, or the follow-up automation?" |
| "We're not ready yet." | "What would need to happen for you to be ready? I ask because most clients tell us they wish they'd started earlier — the system compounds over time." |

## Follow-up sequence (post-assessment call)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Same day | Send summary email: what you discussed, what you'd recommend, and a clear next step |
| Day 3 | Send relevant demo link with a 2-line observation about their specific operation |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp: "Quick check-in — have you had a chance to review? Any questions I can answer?" |
| Day 14 | Value email: case study or insight relevant to their vertical |
| Day 21 | Final follow-up: "I want to be respectful of your time. If now isn't the right moment, no problem — I'll check back in 60 days. Just let me know." |

## Disqualification criteria (do not continue pursuing)

- No budget clarity after two conversations
- Wants results without investment ("do it for a percentage of revenue with no upfront")
- Disrespects the process ("just build me a landing page")
- Chases features, not outcomes
- Cannot make the decision themselves and the decision-maker is never available
- Has unrealistic timeline expectations (wants everything in one week)
